Warehouse Robot Planner

See why robots need to plan in both space and time. Start with the two-robot example, then view the actual 50-robot public instance.

Filled circles: robots; dashed circles: assigned goals; dark squares: walls; pale blue squares: corridor cells. Robots remain on their goals after finishing. Numbers identify robots.

What the planner does
  1. Measure wall-aware shortest distances with BFS.
  2. Construct paths one robot at a time using safe intervals and A*.
  3. Reserve occupied cells, edges and permanently parked goals.
  4. Repair groups using full cell-and-time search, keeping only complete plans with lower exact cost.

The supplied benchmark validates the plans independently. Our method is a heuristic: it does not prove the globally optimal plan and does not guarantee every unseen instance.
